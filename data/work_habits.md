# Work Habits & Engineering Standards

- **Code Quality:** I maintain clean, clear, and professional English syntax across source code comments, terminal logs, and system documentation.
- **Workflow Philosophy:** Modular design—test models and data processing logic independently in Jupyter Notebooks or Google Colab before wrapping them in Flask/Streamlit APIs.
- **Learning & Certification:** Cyber Threat Management certification from Cisco Networking Academy, focusing on network vulnerability management and threat defense strategies.
- **Advice for Junior Students:** Focus on understanding vector representations, semantic chunking, and similarity metrics rather than treating LLMs as magic black boxes.
